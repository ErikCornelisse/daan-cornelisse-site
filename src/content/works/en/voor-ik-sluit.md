As an expert by experience and an artist, I was asked to make a work for a museum exhibition about epilepsy. The artwork “Voor ik sluit” (Before I close) was on show from March to May 2024 in the exhibition “Onzichtbaar Verlies” (Invisible Loss) at the Beautiful Distress House in Amsterdam.

“Onzichtbaar Verlies” reflects on the stigma and uncertainty surrounding epilepsy. A subject close to me, because I have lived with it all my life.
