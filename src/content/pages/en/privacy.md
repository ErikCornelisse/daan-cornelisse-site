This website belongs to Daan Cornelisse. Below you can read which data the site processes and why.

## Contact form

When you use the contact form, your name, e-mail address and message are sent to Daan by e-mail through the [Web3Forms](https://web3forms.com/) service. Daan uses this data only to reply to you and does not share it with anyone else. You can also e-mail Daan directly; then only your own e-mail provider handles your message.

## No cookies, no tracking

The site uses no cookies, no analytics or advertising services, and loads no content from social media. Fonts and images come from the site itself. The links to Instagram, Spotify and LinkedIn are plain links: only when you click one do you reach that service, and its own terms apply.

## Language choice

If you choose Dutch or English with the language switch, your browser remembers that choice on your own device. The choice is not sent to the site.

## Hosting

As with any website, the hosting provider keeps technical log files, such as IP addresses, to keep the site secure and available.

## Your rights

Would you like to know which data Daan holds about you, or have it deleted? E-mail the address on the contact section.
