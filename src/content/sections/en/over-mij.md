### My work

I am curious by nature and always looking for interesting ideas and concepts. This often takes the form of ambitious, innovative works, where the story behind them adds an extra layer. Specialised in 3D design, cinematography and storytelling, I create intriguing images that each carry their own meaning.

From March to May 2024 my work was on show as part of the immersive exhibition “Onzichtbaar Verlies” (Invisible Loss) at the Beautiful Distress House in Amsterdam. “Onzichtbaar Verlies” reflects on the stigma and uncertainty surrounding epilepsy. A subject close to me, because I have lived with it all my life.

### Epilepsy

I think everyone with epilepsy knows how frightening it is not to be in control of your body. I am conscious during my seizures, which can be very frightening. But that awareness is also my greatest strength. It gives me the chance to pause and consider what it means to be alive. Although the seizures affect my life negatively every day, epilepsy is a source of inspiration for me and motivates me to keep developing myself.

The uncertainty of living with my condition makes me try to get everything out of life. With the aim of sharing my passion for art and culture with a wider audience.
